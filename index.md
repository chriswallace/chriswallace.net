    </div>

    <div class="problem-details">
      <p class="problem-body">That's not a knock — it's just how development works when you're moving fast. But AI products are different. The trust you lose in version one is hard to earn back, and the UX decisions baked in early are the hardest to unwind later.</p>
      <ul class="problem-list">
        <li>Conversation flows nobody actually tested with users</li>
        <li>AI responses that confuse instead of help</li>
        <li>No clear pattern for when the AI is wrong or uncertain</li>
        <li>A design system that's already two sprints behind</li>
        <li>User research that never makes it to the people making decisions</li>
      </ul>
      <p class="problem-consequence">None of these are hard to fix individually. The issue is that without someone whose job it is to hold all of it together, they compound.</p>
