# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Future collaborators (founders, studios and teams who might build something with Chris) and peers and friends who already know his work. They come from a link or a name, often on a phone, and read the page once from top to bottom, scrolling at their own pace.

## Product Purpose

chriswallace.net tells Chris Wallace's story as a designer and founder in one scrolling page: the questions he starts from, the experiments he runs, the art he makes with code and AI, the companies he built with friends, and what he is building now (Homebase Assistant). Success is a reader who finishes the story and wants to make something with him.

## Brand Personality

Storytelling, experimentation, micro-animations. The page tells one story a line at a time. Each section shows the work itself (a drawing, a film, a 3D model, a live algorithm) instead of describing it. Small moving details (the mascot, the lit words, the cursor) reward attention.

## Anti-references

- SaaS landing-page templates: hero metric, feature card grids, gradient accents.
- Stock photography or stock illustration.
- AI restyling or image generation over Chris's own artwork. Show the real pieces or the real algorithm.
- Captions and labels on drawings. Only plates of real work carry a frame label.

## Design Principles

- Show the real thing. Real renders, real films, the real renderer, the real model.
- One idea per screen. Each line has the screen to itself, and the scroll sets the pace.
- Motion tells the story. Animation follows the words and never loops for decoration.
- Experiment in public. The page itself is a place to try things.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- WCAG AA contrast for all text.
- Full reduced-motion support: finished drawings show at once and words light without movement.
- Touch, keyboard and reduced-motion readers get native scrolling. Only the mouse wheel is smoothed.
- Every drawing has a still fallback where canvas, WebGL or video cannot run.
